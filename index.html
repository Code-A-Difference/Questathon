How to play

Use your camera for movement controls, or explore with the keyboard demo.

  1. 1

    Choose your settings

    Pick a break length, seated or standing movement, and an effort level. Gentle needs less movement to run.

  2. 2

    Set up your camera

    Allow camera access, then move comfortably for a short calibration. Keep your shoulders, elbows and wrists in view.

  3. 3

    Move to keep running

    Your movement fills the energy meter and keeps the creature behind you. Pause or finish at any time.
